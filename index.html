Welcome

Much of the pine straw harvested in the United States comes from North Florida and South Georgia. Our business is located in the heart of South Georgia — Sylvester, Georgia — right in the middle of prime pine straw country.

We sell premium longleaf pine straw in square bales and round rolls, delivered all over the Southeast United States. Pine straw retail lots and nurseries are our number one buyers, and we supply landscapers, golf courses, schools, HOAs, municipalities and commercial properties right alongside them.

Common Questions

How much does pine straw cost?

Pine straw is priced by the load. What you pay comes down to the quantity and how far it has to travel. Send us your delivery ZIP and roughly what you need and we will get you a real number — not a runaround.

Is there a minimum order?

We sell primarily by the semi-load, and that is where the pricing works best for everybody. Smaller orders are possible depending on the season and where you are. Call and we will tell you straight.

Do you supply retail lots and nurseries?

That is our bread and butter — pine straw lots, nurseries and garden centers are our number one buyers. We run standing orders by the load through the season so nobody sits empty on a Saturday morning.

What makes longleaf worth it?

Long needles knit together into a mat instead of sitting loose, so the bed holds its edge, keeps its color, and does not wash off a slope the first hard rain. More on our longleaf.

Do you install it?

No. We sell and deliver pine straw only. We do not spread or install.

How far will you deliver?

Eight states out of Sylvester, Georgia — see the full service area.
