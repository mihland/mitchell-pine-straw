How delivery works

Straw goes out on a semi. You tell us where it needs to land and whether the site can take a full trailer — gate width, turnaround room, and who is going to unload matters, so mention it up front and we will plan around it. Half loads are available, and standing orders on a schedule are the easiest way for a busy crew to stay stocked through the season.

Do not see your town on the list? Ask anyway. If it is anywhere in the Southeast there is a good chance we can get a load to you.

What it costs to get there

Delivery distance is worked into the load price, so you get a landed number rather than a base price with haulage added later. Send your ZIP with a quote request and we will price it out.
