How delivery works

Southeast Alabama is close enough that smaller orders can sometimes work, depending on the season. Birmingham, Mobile and Huntsville go out as full semi-loads, with half loads available.

Every delivery is scheduled ahead of time. Tell us about your site up front — gate width, turnaround room and who is unloading — and we will plan the drop around it.

Common questions

Is it longleaf or slash?

Longleaf only. We stopped carrying slash, so every bale and every roll is the long-needle straw.

Do you have round rolls?

Yes, longleaf rolls. They suit crews covering big open ground, banks and large common areas.

How do I get a price?

Send your delivery ZIP and roughly how much you expect to move. Price depends on quantity and how far it travels, and distance is built into the load price, so you get a landed number for your site.
