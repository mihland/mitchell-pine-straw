Why longleaf in Florida

Plenty of the straw sold in Florida is slash. It does the job, but the needles are shorter and the bed does not knit together the same way. Longleaf needles commonly run eight to eighteen inches, lock together into a mat, hold a clean edge and keep their color well into the season.

For a retail lot or nursery, that is a premium product your customers can see the difference in — and one more reason for them to buy from you instead of the big-box store.

How delivery works

Most Florida deliveries go out as full semi-loads, with half loads available.

Every delivery is scheduled ahead of time. Tell us about your site up front — gate width, turnaround room and who is unloading — and we will plan the drop around it.

Standing orders are the easiest way to stay stocked through the season. Set the schedule once and adjust it with a phone call.

Common questions

Do you sell slash too?

No. We stopped carrying slash. Every bale we deliver is longleaf.

Can a garden center set up a standing order?

Yes. Tell us your weekly turn in the busy months and we will build a delivery schedule around it.

How do I get a price?

Send your delivery ZIP and roughly how much you expect to move. Price depends on quantity and how far it travels, and distance is built into the load price, so you get a landed number for your site.
