How delivery works

We sell mainly by the semi-load, and half loads are available.

Every delivery is scheduled ahead of time. Tell us about your site up front — gate width, turnaround room and who is unloading — and we will plan the drop around it.

On full semi loads, ask about a trailer drop: we leave the loaded trailer at your lot so your crew can unload on its own time, then swap it for the empty when the next load comes.

Common questions

Is it really all longleaf?

Yes. The needles run long, lock together into a mat, hold a clean edge and keep their rust color well into the season. We stopped carrying slash.

Can we set up a standing order for the season?

Yes. Tell us your weekly turn and we will build the schedule backward from it, then adjust it with a phone call.

How do I get a price?

Send your delivery ZIP and roughly how much you expect to move. Price depends on quantity and how far it travels, and distance is built into the load price, so you get a landed number for your site.
