Why lots buy from us

A retail lot lives or dies on two things: whether the bales look good on the rack, and whether the truck shows up. We hand-bale every bale on metal box balers, so what you stack Monday looks like what you stack in October — tight, square, and easy for your customer to throw in a trunk without it coming apart in the parking lot.

And because we only sell straw off fields we own or lease, we are not waiting on somebody else's rake crew to decide whether you get a load this week.

Standing orders

Standing orders suit most lots well: a set number of loads on a set schedule through the season, adjusted up or down with a phone call. You are not re-negotiating every load, and we are planning our hauling around you instead of around whoever called first.

Tell us your weekly turn and we will build the schedule backward from it.
